Delete Your Avert AI Account

Avert AI · com.santhoshkumar099.avertai

You can delete your Avert AI account and all of the data associated with it at any time. Deletion is permanent and cannot be undone.

Delete from inside the app

This is the fastest way, and it takes effect immediately.

  1. Open Avert AI and go to the Profile tab.
  2. Under Account, tap Delete Account.
  3. Confirm twice. Your account and data are erased and you are signed out.

Request deletion by email

If you no longer have the app installed, email santhoshkumar18916@gmail.com from the address you signed in with, with the subject “Avert AI account deletion request”.

We verify the request comes from the account owner and complete deletion within 30 days. You will get an email confirming it is done.

What gets deleted

Everything below is erased permanently:

  • Your account and sign-in details
  • Your medical profile, conditions, allergies and emergency contacts
  • Medications and medication history
  • Daily check-ins and activity history
  • AI assistant conversations, including any images you sent
  • Family chat messages and family links
  • Emergency alert history

What we keep, and for how long

We retain nothing that identifies you after deletion. Where a record must be kept to meet a legal or accounting obligation — for example a subscription payment receipt, which is held by Google Play or Apple rather than by us — it is kept only for as long as that obligation requires, and it is not linked to your deleted profile.

Subscriptions are billed by Google Play or the App Store, not by Avert AI. Deleting your account does not cancel an active subscription. Cancel it in the Google Play Store or in your Apple subscription settings.
