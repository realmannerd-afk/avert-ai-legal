Privacy Policy

What Avert AI collects, why it collects it, who it reaches, and how to have it deleted.

Last updated 7 September 2026

Avert AI is not a medical device, and it does not replace emergency services. In a real emergency, call your local emergency number directly.

Guidance from the in-app assistant is general information, not a diagnosis or medical advice.

Who is responsible for your data

Avert AI is operated by the individual developer who publishes it on Google Play. Any privacy question, correction, or deletion request goes to the address at the end of this page, and is answered directly.

What is collected, and why

Everything below is collected because a specific feature needs it. Nothing is gathered speculatively, and nothing is collected in the background for profiling.

Collected data
DataWhy
Name, email address and profile photo, from Google Sign-In To create and identify your account. Google Sign-In is the only way to sign in.
Date of birth, sex, conditions, allergies, blood group, medications, critical notes To tailor the assistant to your situation, and to show responders and linked family what matters in an emergency.
Precise location Attached to an SOS so family can find you, and used to list nearby hospitals. Read when you raise an SOS or open the hospital finder — not continuously.
Photos from your camera or library Only images you choose to attach: a profile picture, a photo on an SOS, a medical document, or a picture sent to the assistant.
Microphone audio Only while you record a voice check-in. The audio is transcribed and the transcript is kept.
Messages you write Your conversations with the assistant, and chats with linked family.
Check-ins and activity To show your own history and trends inside the app.
Push notification token To deliver emergency alerts and reminders to your device.

Avert AI does not read your contacts, does not track you across other apps or websites, and carries no advertising and no advertising identifiers.

Who your data reaches

  • Family you link. Linking is deliberate and needs a code you generate or redeem. A linked person receives your SOS alerts — including your location and any photo you attached — and can exchange messages with you. You choose who is linked, and nobody else sees your data.
  • Supabase hosts the database, file storage and backend functions.
  • Google (Gemini) processes the text, voice transcript or image you send the assistant, to produce its reply.
  • Sarvam AI handles speech-to-text and text-to-speech for voice check-ins across the eleven supported Indian languages.
  • Expo delivers push notifications to your device.
  • Mapbox and OpenStreetMap render maps and locate nearby hospitals.

Your data is never sold, rented, or handed to advertisers or data brokers.

How it is protected

Data travels over encrypted HTTPS and is stored on Supabase's managed infrastructure. Row-level security rules mean you can read your own records and only what a linked family member has explicitly been granted. Photos and medical documents sit in private storage and are served through short-lived signed links rather than public URLs.

Keeping and deleting

Your data is kept for as long as your account exists. Inside the app you can delete individual items as you go — messages, whole conversations, medical entries, and documents.

To delete the account itself and everything attached to it, email the address below from the account's own email address. The account and its data are then deleted.

Children

Avert AI is not directed at children under 13 and accounts are not knowingly created for them. A minor may be covered as an at-risk person only through a parent or guardian who manages the account.

Permissions the app asks for

  • Location — SOS alerts and nearby hospitals.
  • Camera and photo library — photos you choose to attach.
  • Microphone — voice check-ins.
  • Notifications — emergency alerts and reminders.

Each is requested only when you first use the feature behind it. Any of them can be declined or later revoked in Android settings, and the rest of the app carries on working without it.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes in a way that matters, the date at the top changes with it and the revised version is published at this address.
